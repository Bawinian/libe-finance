Bridge Finance

Funding for the interval between money you are owed and money you need now.

What this facility is

Bridge finance is short-term credit arranged around a known incoming sum. It is for a business or corporate that has a commitment to meet before that cash arrives, and wants the gap funded without recasting the whole balance sheet.

Who it is for

Businesses and corporates.

Typical uses

  • Cover a payment that falls due before receivables clear.
  • Hold a transaction together while documentation or a larger facility is completed.
  • Fund a defined, time-bound gap rather than an open-ended overdraft.

What happens next

Tell us the amount, the date it is needed, and what settles the bridge. Terms are set after that assessment, not from a published rate card.
